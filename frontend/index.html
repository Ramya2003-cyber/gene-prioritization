LLM-assisted literature mining

Rank the human genes behind any biomedical topic.

Type a disease, process or phenotype. The app reads the most relevant PubMed abstracts, finds the human genes they mention, has an LLM judge the evidence sentence by sentence, and returns the top k with citations.

        How it works

        1. SearchEurope PMC returns the 250 most relevant PubMed abstracts that mention the topic.
        2. AnnotatePubTator3 marks every gene mention and links it to an NCBI Gene ID.
        3. VerifyMyGene.info keeps only confirmed human genes, with official symbols.
        4. ShortlistCandidates are ordered by how many papers mention them.
        5. LLM reviewAn LLM reads the exact sentences and labels each gene core, supporting, peripheral or unrelated, with cited reasons.
        6. RankA transparent weighted score combines the signals; STRING interactions add a small bonus.

        Score

        score = 0.40·literature + 0.40·LLM relevance + 0.15·LLM evidence + 0.05·network

        • Literature: how many of the topic's 250 abstracts mention the gene (log scale, plus a little weight for total mentions), relative to the most-mentioned gene.
        • LLM relevance: the LLM's judgement of the gene's role: core 1, supporting 0.6, peripheral 0.25, unrelated 0.
        • LLM evidence: the average label of up to 3 evidence sentences: direct finding 1, indirect 0.5, background 0.1.
        • Network: high-confidence STRING interactions with the other candidates, relative to the most connected gene. It is kept small on purpose, so "hub" genes cannot outrank genes the literature is actually about.

        Guardrails & limits

        • The LLM only judges sentences it is given; unknown genes, passage IDs or labels are dropped and reported, never trusted.
        • No gene is invented: if fewer than k human genes are found, you get fewer.
        • Scores rank literature evidence; they are not probabilities of causality.
        • Only a relevance-ordered sample of abstracts is read, so rare genes can be missed.
        Data: Europe PMC · PubTator3 · MyGene.info · STRING. For research exploration only, not clinical use.
